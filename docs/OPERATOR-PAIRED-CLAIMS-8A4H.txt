# Operator 8A.4H — paired simulation admission and freshness fencing

The paired-worker service now exposes trusted internal selectSimulation, claimSimulation and finishSimulation methods. They are not HTTP or browser endpoints. A caller must already establish the private owner scope; no public user input grants authority. The exact current active pairing, account, credential and grant are checked again under the same registry mutex/transaction as selection or reservation. No grant, corrupted credential, replaced pairing, inactive owner or revocation means no selection/claim/completion. A denied guard durably fences held synthetic occurrences. Existing reference-engine API remains available to isolated tests with its original 90-second registration window.

Paired selection and claims require a signed report less than 25 seconds old, not merely an unexpired 90-second registration lease. Claims expire at the earliest of report freshness, registration lease and the 30-second synthetic reservation bound. Subsequent heartbeats cannot extend an already issued claim or resurrect an expired occurrence. Claims bind the exact worker boot/generation and owner, are exclusive and cannot be replayed as a new occurrence. Completion checks the exact claim ID and fresh paired authority. A revoked or expired reservation becomes uncertain; it is not automatically repeated. The wrapper reports mode=simulation, execution_enabled=false and live_certified=false.

SQLite and MySQL fixtures cover cross-owner completion, duplicate reservations, incorrect claim IDs, paired revocation, stale reports, expiry before the longer registration lease, heartbeat recovery and retirement. No scheduler, real contact, media, Twilio dispatch, paid action or LiveKit change is introduced. The authoritative live call admission and contact consent integration are separate later gates. No actual worker has been provisioned.
