OPERATOR SPECIALIST — LIVE CONVERSATION INTELLIGENCE V1

Builds on live caller captions. In an answered private Phone call, explicitly confirm that everyone consents to live Cloud AI transcription. The two-sided option is off by default and additionally requires agreement to process both the existing remote and already-owned local audio tracks. It does not request another microphone or transmit any new audio into the call. Muting the local track stops two-sided capture. Labels describe audio tracks, not verified speaker identities.

Two-sided capture uses aligned four-second PCM frames, independently transcribed for the two sides. Each pair reserves eight speech seconds under the existing 120-second per-call and 240-second rolling-hour speech limits. Consequently both-sided capture lasts at most 60 wall-clock seconds; remote-only remains at most 120 seconds. Slow processing skips frames. Authority is rechecked between provider requests and before returning results. Stop, revoked consent, changed permissions, changed call/Chat, mute in dual mode and capture loss fence new dispatch and late results. Previously dispatched requests may incur charges.

The private response suggestion option is separate and default-off. Ask everyone before enabling it: recent captions are sent to the owner's configured AI model only on the owner's explicit Suggest a response request. The server authenticates short-lived caption evidence with per-session receipts, not client-invented transcripts. It includes at most three recent same-peer/assigned-Agent call metadata records; no saved report content, contact name or phone number is retrieved for model context. Caption speech itself may contain private information. The model uses the existing account inference and quota path, receives no tools, and returns a short text-only suggestion. No speech, call, Calendar entry, message, task or other external action is executed. Suggestions are capped at two attempts per call, eight per rolling hour, at least twenty seconds apart, with fresh caption evidence.

Captions and suggestions are transient; the additional table stores authorization mode, a server-only receipt key and attempt counters, not conversation text or audio. Explicit upgrade adds the metadata table; manual phone calling remains available before upgrade. Existing post-call recording/transcription and reports are unchanged.

Verification: SQLite/MySQL exact-call scope, dual consent, PCM validation, budget reservation, revocation between two speech requests, forged receipt rejection, no unconsented model inference, rate limits and late model response suppression; four-rate AudioWorklet regression; browser test uses separate synthetic audio tracks and fixture speech/model responses. Full CI and actual carrier/device/provider testing remain required. Agent audio injection, turn-taking, takeover and autonomous dialing are not enabled in this release.
