VP3 MUSIC WORKSPACE ANALYTICS — ML3

This release adds artist-scoped listening reports to /music-workspace.php and
extends safe public-page attribution for connected Stoneward artist sites.
VP3 remains the canonical catalog/audio provider. Artist earnings and payments
are not activated by this update.

INSTALL

1. Back up matching Cloud code, database, config.php, private/ and uploads/ before
   applying the source-verified full Cloud deployment ZIP. Preserve the existing
   configuration, private keys, uploads and database. Read VP3_RELEASE.json and
   verify the source commit and archive checksum from the release handoff.

2. The dashboard itself adds no new database schema and performs no read-path
   migration. Its catalog, native listening and provider prerequisites are the
   existing ML1/ML2A/ML2B upgrades. If those are absent, follow the matching
   MUSIC-CATALOG-LISTENING-DEPLOY.txt and MUSIC-PROVIDER-DEPLOY.txt procedures.
   A schema-unavailable report is not evidence of zero listening.

3. Sign in as the workspace owner or authorized manager and open Music Workspace.
   Confirm the selected workspace and reporting timezone, then choose a date
   period. Existing song, stem, release, Player and team tools remain available.

4. The default Audience view excludes known artist previews and staging tests.
   Anonymous production visitors remain unclassified. Use the explicit all-
   traffic/legacy controls to inspect older or test evidence. Ordinary artist
   reports do not expose individual listeners or their personal history.

5. Update the paired Stoneward site with its matching verified release to send
   public track/release page context. Either update order remains compatible:
   older providers retain site/song attribution but omit the new page paths;
   older clients continue their previous supported route profile. The exact
   tested VP3 and Stoneward commits belong in the release handoff.

READING THE REPORT

The selected period means sessions begun during those local calendar dates.
Listening totals are their accepted cumulative measurements at the displayed
snapshot time. A play spanning midnight belongs to its start-date group, and
ongoing plays can update a previous date. This is not an exact minute-by-minute
allocation of all audio heard during the period.

Native measurement, connected-site measurement and legacy-only history are
distinct. A versioned native session's compatibility row is excluded from the
legacy total, so it is not counted twice. A playback request alone does not
establish audible playback. Qualified engagement retains the versioned 10-second
definition; completion uses accepted time against 80% of a usable duration.
Unknown duration does not become zero or a known completion result.

Provider sessions are not unique people. Source pages are reported by clients;
site registration verifies the origin, not the visitor or their attention. Public
page URLs omit query strings/fragments. Unsupported/private paths stay unknown.
Audio delivery bytes describe server usage and are separate from listening.

Presets cover 7/30/90 dates. Custom ranges are bounded to 366 dates and the initial
report to 25,000 source sessions. If the limit is exceeded, narrow the filters or
period. No incomplete total is presented as a complete report. Breakdown paging
does not change the total.

ML3A adds a separate All-time listening panel with SQL aggregates over all retained
measured history. It has no lower date boundary or 25,000-session materialization
limit. Its source/traffic filters are independent of this dated report. Read
MUSIC-LISTENING-TOTALS-DEPLOY.txt for retained-history and platform-total semantics.

Earnings and Payments show Not configured until the later financial phases
establish funding, rates/agreements, eligibility, statements and settlement.

PILOT AND RECOVERY

Test one song across the actual VP3 and Stoneward hosts: verify the registered
site, import/reimport without duplication, play/seek/pause/renew, inspect one
correlated listening record and public page source, then withdraw/revoke access.
Exercise desktop and physical iPhone/Android playback and actual hosting/CDN
headers. This remains the ML2D live gate; automated browser tests alone do not
certify it.

For rollback, restore matching code/configuration/database/private data/uploads
as a recoverable set. No analytics read should write listening or schema. Keep
the production provider installation identity and signing key with its database.
Do not clone private site credentials into another artist installation.
