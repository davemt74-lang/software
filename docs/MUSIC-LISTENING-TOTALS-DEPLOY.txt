VP3 ALL-TIME LISTENING TOTALS — ML3A

This update adds combined native VP3 and connected-site listening totals to
/music-workspace.php and /admin/listening.php. It uses existing retained listening
records. The Stoneward streaming protocol and the 10-second listen rule remain
compatible with the already verified Stoneward v1.3.333 release.

INSTALL

1. Back up matching code, database, config.php, private/ and uploads/. Verify the
   full Cloud ZIP checksum and its VP3_RELEASE.json commit against the release
   handoff. Preserve configuration, private keys, uploaded media and database.

2. This reporting update adds no schema or mutable counter and performs no
   read-path migration. Existing ML1 catalog, ML2A native listening and ML2B
   provider prerequisites still apply. If those are absent, follow the matching
   MUSIC-CATALOG-LISTENING-DEPLOY.txt and MUSIC-PROVIDER-DEPLOY.txt notes.

3. Open the authorized Music Workspace. All-time listening shows retained 10s+
   listens for the workspace and each canonical song. Its source/traffic filters
   are independent of the dated report below it. Page through songs and confirm
   the headline total stays the same. Zero-listen catalog songs remain visible.

4. With current listening.view permission, open Admin > Listening Analytics.
   The combined platform total includes native and connected-site measurements.
   Open an artist workspace to inspect song totals within platform scope.
   The older native/legacy report and its date controls remain separately labeled.
   Do not add those older figures to the combined total.

5. No Stoneward update is required for this reporting follow-up. Existing sites
   continue sending measured sessions through the existing VP3 provider API.

WHAT THE TOTAL MEANS

All-time means all retained measured history through the displayed snapshot.
There is no lower date boundary and no dated-report 25,000-session limit. The
database aggregates the full retained history; song/artist lists are paged without
changing totals. This has not been benchmarked against an actual production
database's unmeasured size or host resources.

One listen requires at least 10,000 accepted playing milliseconds in one logical
session. Retry receipts do not allocate another session. A stream request alone
does not prove a start. Completion uses accepted time against 80% of known usable
duration. Unknown duration is not a known completion result.

Audience excludes known artist/manager previews, private previews and staging
tests. Anonymous production visitors are not verified independent people. The
All traffic view includes measured test classes and separately labeled legacy-only
session/time figures. Native compatibility rows are excluded from legacy totals.
Legacy records do not become newly measured listens, unique people or earnings.

Removed history cannot be recovered by this report. Older native track/history
deletion can cascade into native session deletion, and listening before tracking
was installed may never have been recorded. Canonical archives and site revocation
retain historical counts when their song/workspace/site associations remain intact.
Still-retained unassociated or ambiguous evidence is disclosed in coverage.

Missing-source warnings mean the total covers only available selected sources.
An unavailable source is not a measured zero. An explicit unavailable-source
request, unsupported metric version or out-of-range aggregate fails visibly.
Ordinary reports disclose no individual listeners or their private history.

Artist authority and platform listening.view authority remain separate. Removing
membership, permission or account access invalidates further reports. No report
activates an earning rate, financial eligibility, statement, balance or payment.
Earnings and Payments remain Not configured.

VERIFY AND RECOVER

After applying the package, compare a known native listen and a known connected
site listen against the appropriate source filters, then verify the combined
song/workspace total and platform drilldown. This is a host-side verification
step; the release's automated fixtures do not claim your host was deployed.

The actual-host and physical iPhone/Android streaming pilot remains ML2D. Preserve
production provider installation identity and signing keys with the database.
Rollback uses the matching code/configuration/database/private/uploads backup.
The new report never writes schema, counters or listening events.
